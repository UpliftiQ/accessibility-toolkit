# Governance

## Current model

UpliftiQ is the project steward during the pre-1.0 stage. Maintainers manage scope, releases, security and community standards. The roadmap and technical decisions remain public.

## Decision principles

Decisions are evaluated in this order:

1. Safety, consent and privacy
2. Deaf and hard-of-hearing user benefit
3. Accessibility across different needs
4. Web standards and interoperability
5. API clarity and long-term maintenance
6. Implementation convenience

## Decision process

- Small fixes use normal issue and pull-request review.
- New components and breaking API changes begin with a public proposal.
- Major caption, transcript or sign-language UX changes require relevant community review.
- Maintainers publish the decision, reasoning, trade-offs and known limitations.
- When consensus is not possible, the project lead makes the final decision and records why.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainers are selected from consistent contributors who demonstrate technical care, respectful community work and accessibility judgement. A future public maintainer policy will define nomination, review and removal after the contributor community is established.

## Conflicts of interest

Maintainers must disclose employment, financial or organisational interests that could materially influence a decision. UpliftiQ’s private products do not receive undocumented exceptions from public package rules.
