# Community code of conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a community where people can contribute without harassment or exclusion. We welcome differences in hearing identity, sign language, disability, race, caste, ethnicity, gender, age, religion, education, geography, technical experience and communication style.

## Expected behaviour

- Communicate clearly and patiently.
- Give people time to read, sign, caption, interpret or respond.
- Caption shared video and describe essential visuals.
- Accept correction about language, accessibility and lived experience.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Ask before sharing someone’s signed video, story or personal information.
- Credit community expertise as real expertise.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, discrimination or sexualised attention
- Mocking language, signing, writing style, disability or assistive technology
- Publishing private information without consent
- Using signed media outside its documented permission
- Deliberately introducing accessibility barriers
- Persistent disruption after a maintainer asks for behaviour to stop

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to `hi@upliftiq.in`. Maintainers will review reports, protect confidentiality where possible and respond in proportion to the behaviour. Actions may include a warning, content removal, temporary restriction or permanent removal from project spaces.

Project maintainers are responsible for applying this policy consistently, including to other maintainers.
